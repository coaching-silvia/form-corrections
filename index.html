Applies to every exercise

  • Tempo: about 2–3 s down, pause where asked, about 1 s up
  • Keep tension: don't rest the weight between reps; rest only at the end of the set
  • Get close to failure: at 12 clean reps, add weight and drop back to 8
  • Technique before weight: if form breaks down on the last reps, stop or go lighter
  • Breathing: out on the effort, in on the return
  • Film from the side and from further away (0.5× wide angle if your phone has it), so your back is visible
  • Rest days: at least 1 day between full-body sessions. Two days in a row only as lower body + upper body
  • If you skip part of a session, don't go back the next day to finish it (15/10)
  • Weekly check-in by Friday, Saturday morning at the latest: the coaches review it together on Saturday (24/10)

Hip thrust

Glutes · bar / Smith / machine

Corrections

What "drive up with your glutes" means (coach's answer, 26/10): as you come up, squeeze your glutes and let them lift you. Your upper body rises at the same time as your hips. In your videos your back went backwards first and your glutes followed, so your back was saving the weight. Your back must not reach the bench before your hips come up.

⚠︎ No extra weight for now (21/10): fix hip height at the top first.
